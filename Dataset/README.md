🏠 In 2020, property sales didn't just grow. They nearly doubled in a single year.

I was going through 526,276 real estate transactions across 50 towns and 11 years — and when I hit the 2020 number, I genuinely had to check it twice.

Sales jumped from $22.1 Billion in 2019 to $40.3 Billion in 2020. An 82% surge. During a pandemic. That one number changed how I read everything else in the data.

📊 What the full picture looks like:

→ The market grew 231% between 2011 and 2020 — from $12.17B to $40.29B in a single year
→ 57% of all transactions are Single Family homes — people want space, not apartments
→ March consistently shows the highest month-on-month growth at +30% every year
→ 2021 sales stayed 38% above pre-pandemic levels — this is not a temporary spike, it is a new normal
